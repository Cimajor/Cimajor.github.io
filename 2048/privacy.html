2048 — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains information handled when you use 2048 (the “App”, package ID com.illiamilevskiy.game2048). 2048 is developed by Arcade GameDev. Questions may be sent to arcadegamedevt@gmail.com.

Local app data

2048 does not require an account and Arcade GameDev does not collect a user profile. Scores, sound preference, selected theme, ad cooldown, and a token-free cache of ownership previously confirmed by Google Play are stored locally on your device. The App does not store payment-card data or purchase tokens.

Google services

Google AdMob provides interstitial and player-initiated rewarded advertising. Under Google’s policies, AdMob may handle advertising ID or device/account identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, app interactions, and diagnostics. See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner sites and apps.

Google Play Billing supplies localized product information and reconciles ownership of 2048 Premium. Google may process purchase identifiers, purchase state, acknowledgement state, and a purchase token for that purpose. Google controls payment and purchase records; Arcade GameDev does not receive card data or directly process refunds.

Consent and advertising

The App requests consent information through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) and follows UMP’s canRequestAds result and runtime controls. A Privacy Options control is available only when UMP reports that it is required. UMP and Google’s applicable consent, eligibility, account, device, and regional controls determine ad eligibility; the App does not force a region-specific personalized or non-personalized ad mode.

2048 Premium

game2048_premium is a non-consumable, one-time product at the localized price Google Play displays before confirmation. Confirmed ownership unlocks the Alchemy, Prism, and Bauhaus themes, removes interstitials, and lets you choose the optional once-per-game Get Extra Move reward without loading or watching an ad. The reward remains optional and is never granted automatically. Google Play manages payment and eligible-account ownership reconciliation.

Children, retention, and choices

The final Play target-audience declaration and Data Safety answers must match the shipped build and Play Console configuration. Arcade GameDev does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Because Arcade GameDev does not store personal data or operate an App account, there is no developer-held profile to delete. Google controls data it processes through its services; its controls and policies explain available choices.

Changes and contact

This policy may change; the effective date will identify the latest revision. Contact arcadegamedevt@gmail.com with privacy questions.
