Retro Arcade Collection — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains information handled when you use Retro Arcade Collection (the “App”, package ID com.illiamilevskiy.retroarcadecollection). Retro Arcade Collection is developed by Arcade GameDev. Questions may be sent to arcadegamedevt@gmail.com.

Local app data

Retro Arcade Collection does not require an account and Arcade GameDev does not collect a user profile. Gameplay progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

Google advertising services

Google AdMob provides advertising. Under Google’s policies, AdMob may handle advertising ID or device/account identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, app interactions, and diagnostics. See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner sites and apps.

Optional usage analytics

If you choose Allow analytics, Brick Game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how players use the game and improve it. Analytics measures app launches and sessions, which game modes you play, play duration, scores, completed or abandoned runs, advertising delivery and advertising revenue. The SDK also processes an app-instance identifier and technical information such as app version, operating system, device model, language and approximate geographic information. These records help us understand returning players and where players stop playing. We do not send your name, email address or a custom account ID.

Analytics is optional and is off until you allow it. You can change your choice in Settings → Usage analytics. Turning it off disables further collection and resets locally stored Analytics data; it does not delete data already processed by Google. If you enable it again, Analytics may assign a new app-instance identifier. This setting is separate from advertising privacy choices, which remain available through the existing advertising privacy controls.

The Analytics integration disables advertising-ID collection and does not enable ad personalization or advertising-data consent. Advertising SDK processing is described separately in this policy.

See Google’s privacy information and Firebase privacy and security information.

Consent and advertising

The App requests consent information through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) and follows UMP’s canRequestAds result and runtime controls. A Privacy Options control is available only when UMP reports that it is required. UMP and Google’s applicable consent, eligibility, account, device, and regional controls determine ad eligibility; the App does not force a region-specific personalized or non-personalized ad mode.

Children, retention, and choices

The final Play target-audience declaration and Data Safety answers must match the shipped build and Play Console configuration. Arcade GameDev does not knowingly collect personal data from children. The App does not offer user accounts. Optional analytics data is processed through Google Analytics for Firebase as described above. You can stop future analytics collection in Settings and contact us with questions or requests about previously collected data. Google also provides controls and policies for data processed through its services.

Changes and contact

This policy may change; the effective date will identify the latest revision. Contact arcadegamedevt@gmail.com with privacy questions.
